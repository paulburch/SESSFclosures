DIRECTION NOT TO ENGAGE IN FISHING
Fisheries Management Act 1991
Subsections 41A(3)
SOUTHERN AND EASTERN SCALEFISH AND SHARK FISHERY (Closure Revocation) DIRECTION No. 5 2010
I, James Findlay, Chief Executive Officer of the Australian Fisheries Management Authority, as delegate under delegation No. 1 of 2011 made on 28th January 2011 from the Commission, make the following Direction under section 41A(3) of the Fisheries Management Act 1991.
Dated: 3 March 2012
James Findlay
Chief Executive Officer
Australian Fisheries Management Authority
Citation
This Direction may be cited as the Southern and Eastern Scalefish and Shark Fishery (Closure Revocation) Direction No. 5 2010.
Commencement
2. This Direction commences on 7 March 2012.
Cessation
3. This Direction ceases on 30 June 2012.
Revocation of previous Direction
4. This Direction revokes the following Direction:
Southern and Eastern Scalefish and Shark Fishery (Closures) Direction No. 5 2010 (F2011C00878).
