FISHERIES MANAGEMENT ACT 1991
SOUTHERN AND EASTERN SCALEFISH AND SHARK FISHERY MANAGEMENT PLAN 2003
SOUTHERN AND EASTERN SCALEFISH AND SHARK FISHERY (Closures) DIRECTION No. 4 2010
(revoking SESSF (Closures) Direction No. 2 2010)
PROHIBITION ON FISHING
I, James Findlay, A/g Chief Executive Officer of the Australian Fisheries Management Authority, as delegate, make the following Direction under section 17(5A) of the Fisheries Management Act 1991 and section 56(1) of the Southern and Eastern Scalefish and Shark Fishery Management Plan 2003.
Dated 1 December 2010
Signed – Dr James Findlay
A/g Chief Executive Officer,
Australian Fisheries Management Authority
------------------------------------------
1. Citation
This Direction may be cited as SESSF (Closures) Direction No. 4 2010
2. Commencement
This Direction commences at the start of 17 December 2010.
3. Application
This Direction applies to all concession holders in the Southern and Eastern Scalefish and Shark Fishery and to a person acting on behalf of the holder.
4. Period of Application
This Direction applies for a period beginning on the date this Direction commences and ending on 30 April 2016.
5. Definitions
An expression used in this direction has the same meaning as in the Southern and Eastern Scalefish and Shark Fishery Management Plan 2003.
“Adjacent Area Boundary” means the offshore State and Northern Territory boundaries established under Schedule 2 of the Petroleum and Submerged Lands Act 1967 as amended.
All geographic coordinates contained within the schedules attached to this document are in terms of the Geocentric Datum of Australia 1994 (GDA94).
Automatic baiting equipment - any mechanical operation that automatically positions bait on the hooks without the need for each hook/snood to be individually clipped onto the mainline directly by hand during the setting of the gear.
Unless otherwise stated, the lines between coordinates are geodesics.
6. Direction not to engage in fishing
A person in:
(a) The area described in Schedule 1 is not to engage in fishing using gillnet methods.
(b) The area described by Schedule 2 is not to engage in fishing using hook methods.
(c) The area described by Schedule 3 is not to engage in fishing.
(d) The area described by Schedule 4 is not to engage in fishing using demersal otter trawl gear.
(e) The area described by Schedule 5 is not to engage in fishing using trawl methods.
(f) An area described in Schedule 6, 7 and 8 is not to engage in fishing.
(g) The area described in Schedule 9 is not to engage in fishing using trawl methods.
(h) The area described in Schedule 10 is not to engage in fishing using gillnet methods.
(i) The area described by Schedule 11 is not to engage in fishing.
(j) The area described by Schedule 12 is not to engage in fishing.
(k) The area described in Schedule 13 is not to engage in fishing using
(i) gillnet methods in waters deeper than 183m; and
(ii) hook methods if the person would be fishing under the authority of a shark hook boat statutory fishing right in waters deeper than 183m.
(l) The area described in Schedule 14 is not to engage in fishing using:
(i) gillnet methods; and
(ii) hook methods if the person would be fishing under the authority of a shark hook boat statutory fishing right.
(m) The area described in Schedule 15 is not to engage in fishing using automatic baiting equipment in waters shallower than 183m.
(n) The area described in Schedule 16 is not to engage in fishing using hook methods.
(o) The area described in Schedule 17 is not to engage in fishing using demersal otter trawl gear.
(p) The area described in Schedule 18 is not to engage in fishing.
(q) The area described in Schedule 19 is not to engage in fishing using trawl or hook methods.
(r) The area described in Schedule 20 is not to engage in fishing using trawl methods.
(s) The area described in Schedule 21 is not to engage in fishing using trawl methods.
(t) The area described in Schedule 22 is not to engage in fishing using demersal otter trawl gear.
(u) The area described in Schedule 23 is not to engage in fishing using demersal otter trawl gear.
(v) An area described in Schedules 24, 25 and 26 is not to engage in fishing using demersal otter trawl gear.
(w) An area described in Schedule 27 - 35 is not to engage in fishing using trawl methods.
(x) The area described in Schedule 36 is not to engage in fishing using trawl methods.
(y) The area described in Schedule 37 is not to engage in fishing.
(z) The area described in Schedule 38 is not to engage in fishing..
(aa) The area described in Schedule 39 is not to engage in fishing.
(bb) The area described in Schedule 40 is not to engage in fishing.
7. Revocation of SESSF (Closures) Direction No. 2 2010
This Direction revokes SESSF (Closures) Direction No. 2 2010 with effect from the commencement of this Direction.
