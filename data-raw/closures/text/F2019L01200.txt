Southern and Eastern Scalefish and Shark Fishery (Closures Variation) Direction (No. 2) 2019
I, Wesley Norris, Chief Executive Officer of the Australian Fisheries Management Authority, make the following direction.
Dated 16 September 2019
Wesley Norris
Chief Executive Officer
1 Name
This instrument is the Southern and Eastern Scalefish and Shark Fishery (Closures Variation) Direction (No. 2) 2019.
2 Commencement
This instrument commences on 16 September 2019.
3 Authority
This instrument is made under subsection 41A(3) of the Fisheries Management Act 1991 and subsection 33(3) of the Acts Interpretation Act 1901.
4 Schedules
Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned.
Schedule 1—Repeals
Southern and Eastern Scalefish and Shark Fishery (Closures Variation) Direction 2019 (F2019L00650)
1 The whole of the instrument
Repeal the instrument with the provisions repealed by the instrument being revived on 16 September 2019 as if the repealing provisions had not been made.
