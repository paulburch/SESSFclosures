FISHERIES MANAGEMENT ACT 1991
SOUTHERN AND EASTERN SCALEFISH AND SHARK FISHERY MANAGEMENT PLAN 2003
SOUTHERN AND EASTERN SCALEFISH AND SHARK FISHERY (Closures) DIRECTION 2008
(revoking SESSF (Closures) Direction June 2007)
PROHIBITION ON FISHING
The Australian Fisheries Management Authority makes the following Direction under section 17(5A) of the Fisheries Management Act 1991 and section 56(1) of the Southern and eastern Scalefish and Shark Fishery Management Plan 2003.
Dated 30 June 2008
The Common seal of the Australian
Fisheries Management Authority
was affixed in accordance with a
resolution of the Board
Peter Witheridge
------------------------------------------
1. Citation
This Direction may be cited as SESSF (Closures) Direction 2008
2. Commencement
This Direction commences the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislative Instruments.
3. Application
This Direction applies to a holder of statutory fishing rights in the Southern and Eastern Scalefish and Shark Fishery and to a person acting on behalf of the holder.
4. Period of Application
This Direction applies for a period beginning on the date this Direction commences and ending on 1 January 2012.
5. Definitions
An expression used in this direction has the same meaning as in the Southern and Eastern Scalefish and Shark Fishery Management Plan 2003.
Automatic baiting equipment - any mechanical operation that automatically positions bait on the hooks without the need for each hook/snood to be individually clipped onto the mainline directly by hand during the setting of the gear.
6. Prohibition on fishing
A person in:
(a) An area described in Schedule 1 is prohibited from fishing using gillnet methods.
(b) An area described by Schedule 2 is prohibited from fishing using hook methods.
(c) An area described by Schedule 3 is prohibited from fishing.
(d) An area described by Schedule 4 is prohibited from fishing using demersal otter trawl gear .
(e) An area described by Schedule 5 is prohibited from fishing using trawl methods.
(f) An area described in Schedule 6, 7 and 8 is prohibited from fishing.
(g) An area described in Schedule 9 is prohibited from fishing using trawl methods.
(h) An area described in Schedule 10 is prohibited from fishing using gillnet methods.
(i) An area described by Schedule 11 is prohibited from fishing.
(j) An area described by Schedule 12 is prohibited from fishing.
(k) An area described in Schedule 13 is prohibited from fishing using gillnet methods.
(l) An area described in Schedule 14 is prohibited from fishing using gillnet methods or holding a permit for the Tasmanian Coastal Waters Sector.
(m) An area described in Schedule 15 is prohibited from fishing using automatic baiting equipment.
(n) An area described in Schedule 16 is prohibited from fishing using hook methods.
(o) An area described in Schedule 17 is prohibited from fishing using demersal otter trawl gear.
(p) An area described in Schedule 18 is prohibited from fishing.
(q) An area described in Schedule 19 is prohibited from fishing using trawl or hook methods.
(r) An area described in Schedule 20 is prohibited from fishing using trawl methods.
(s) An area described in Schedule 21 is prohibited from fishing using trawl methods.
(t) An area described in Schedule 22 is prohibited from fishing using demersal otter trawl gear.
(u) An area described in Schedule 23 is prohibited from fishing using demersal otter trawl gear.
(v) An area described in Schedules 24, 25 and 26 is prohibited from fishing using demersal otter trawl gear.
(w) An area described in Schedule 27 - 35 is prohibited from fishing using trawl methods.
7. To whom do the prohibitions in clause 6 not apply?
The prohibitions in clause six do not apply to a person if the person is fishing in accordance with:
another plan of management in force under the Fisheries Management Act 1991 , or a law of a State or Territory that is in force in relation to the Southern and Eastern Scalefish and Shark Fishery area; or
II. a scientific permit which gives specific conditions enabling the holder to operate in the specified area of waters.
8. Revocation of SESSF Closures Direction 2007
This Direction revokes SESSF (Closures) Direction 2007 with effect from the commencement of this Direction.
