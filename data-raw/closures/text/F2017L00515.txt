DIRECTION NOT TO ENGAGE IN FISHING
Fisheries Management Act 1991
Subsection 41A(3)
SOUTHERN AND EASTERN SCALEFISH AND SHARK FISHERY (CLOSURES) DIRECTION REVOCATION 2017
I, James Findlay, Chief Executive Officer of the Australian Fisheries Management Authority, as delegate under Fisheries Delegation No. 2 of 2016 made on 14 November 2016 from the Commission, make the following Direction under subsection 41A(3) of the Fisheries Management Act 1991.
Dated: 8 May 2017
James Findlay
Chief Executive Officer
Australian Fisheries Management Authority
Citation
This Direction may be cited as the Southern and Eastern Scalefish and Shark Fishery (Closures) Direction Revocation 2017.
Commencement
2. This Direction commences on the day after it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.
Revocation of previous Direction
3. This Direction revokes the Southern and Eastern Scalefish and Shark Fishery (Closures) Direction (No. 1) 2014 with effect from the commencement of this Direction.
