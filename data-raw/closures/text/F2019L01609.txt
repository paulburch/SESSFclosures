Fisheries (Southern and Eastern Scalefish and Shark Fishery Closures) Revocation Direction 2019
I, Wez Norris, Chief Executive Officer of the Australian Fisheries Management Authority, make the following direction.
Dated 11 December 2019
Wez Norris
Chief Executive Officer
1 Name
This instrument is the Fisheries (Southern and Eastern Scalefish and Shark Fishery Closures) Revocation Direction 2019.
2 Commencement
(1) Each provision of this instrument specified in column 1 of the table commences, or is taken to have commenced, in accordance with column 2 of the table. Any other statement in column 2 has effect according to its terms.

3 Authority
This instrument is made under subsection 41A(3) of the Fisheries Management Act 1991.
4 Schedules
Each instrument that is specified in a Schedule to this instrument is repealed as set out in the applicable items in the Schedule concerned, and any other item in a Schedule to this instrument has effect according to its terms.
