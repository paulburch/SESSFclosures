DIRECTION NOT TO ENGAGE IN FISHING
Fisheries Management Act 1991
Subsection 41A(2)
SOUTHERN AND EASTERN SCALEFISH AND SHARK FISHERY (CLOSURES) DIRECTION No. 1 2017
I, James Findlay, Chief Executive Officer of the Australian Fisheries Management Authority, as delegate under No. 2 of 2016 made on 14th November 2016 from the Commission, make the following Direction under subsection 41A(2) of the Fisheries Management Act 1991.
Dated: 11 September 2017
James Findlay
Chief Executive Officer
Australian Fisheries Management Authority
1 Name
This Instrument may be cited as the Southern and Eastern Scalefish and Shark Fishery (Closures) Direction No. 1 2017.
2 Commencement
This Instrument commences the day it is registered on the Federal Register of Legislation.
3 Definitions
(1) In this instrument:
Act means the Fisheries Management Act 1991.
Management Plan means the Southern and Eastern Scalefish and Shark Fishery Management Plan 2003.
(2) A term used in this Instrument that is defined in the Management Plan has the same meaning in this Instrument as it has in the Management Plan.
4 Repeal
This Instrument is repealed on 9 March 2019, unless earlier revoked.
5 Application
This Instrument applies to a holder, in the Southern and Eastern Scalefish and Shark Fishery, of either or both:
(a) a Gillnet Boat Statutory Fishing Right
(b) a South Australian Coastal Waters Permit
and to any person acting on behalf of the holder.
Note: This means that the Direction does not apply to a person if the person is fishing in the area of the Fishery in accordance with a fishing concession that is subject to another plan of management in force under the Act, under a scientific permit, a permit issued in another fishery, or under a licence issued under a law of a State or Territory.
6 Direction not to engage in fishing
Fishing by gillnet methods is not to be engaged in in the areas of waters of the Southern and Eastern Scalefish and Shark Fishery described in Schedule 1.
7 Interaction with other legislative instruments
This Instrument does not affect any other Direction made under section 41A of Act.
Schedule 1
Southern and Eastern Scalefish and Shark Fishery (Closures) Direction No. 1 2017.
Fishing by gillnet methods is not to be engaged in in the following area:
Australian Sea Lion Management Strategy Adaptive Management Zone D

(a) commencing at the intersection of the mean low water line of the southern coastline of South Australia at the point 33° 43' 34” south by 135° 00' 00” east;
